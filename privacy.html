Privacy Policy

Lumiere AI Photo Editor · Last updated: August 27, 2026
Summary: Lumiere is designed to minimize personal data collection. No account is required for the core app. Some AI features and advertising services may process information as described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumiere AI Photo Editor (“Lumiere”, “we”, “us”, “our”, or the “App”) handles information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

  • Photos and media: We may access photos or media that you explicitly select for editing.
  • Camera: If you grant camera permission and use the camera feature, the App may access images you capture.
  • AI prompts: Text instructions you submit to AI features may be processed to provide the requested result.
  • Random installation identifier: The App may create a random per-install identifier to enforce fair usage limits for cloud AI features. It is not intended to directly identify you.
  • Advertising information: Advertising providers may collect device, advertising, and usage information as described below.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide photo editing and AI features you request.
  • Process selected images and prompts when you intentionally use cloud AI features.
  • Apply usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Photos, Camera, and Media

The App may request photo/media and camera permissions so you can select or capture content. Granting permission does not by itself mean that your photos are uploaded.

Local editing features may process images on your device. When you intentionally use a cloud AI feature requiring an image, the selected image may be transmitted to the configured AI backend over HTTPS to perform the requested operation.

5. AI and Cloud Processing

Some features, such as AI image generation, AI-assisted editing, and image upscaling, may use cloud processing. When you request such a feature, relevant prompts and/or selected images may be sent to the AI backend configured for the App over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Cloud infrastructure may temporarily process or retain technical request information as necessary to provide and secure the service. Retention may also depend on third-party infrastructure providers.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

Lumiere may use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and usage information, subject to applicable settings and consent choices.

For more information about Google's practices, please review Google's applicable privacy documentation.

7. Permissions

  • Photos/Media: selecting and saving images.
  • Camera: capturing photos when you choose to use the camera.
  • Internet: cloud AI, advertising, and network services.
  • Notifications: if enabled and permitted, relevant app notifications.

You can manage permissions in Android settings. Some features may not work when a required permission is denied.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the App, including AI infrastructure providers, Google AdMob, hosting/network providers, and other technical providers.

9. Data Retention

We do not intentionally create a personal profile of users for the core operation of the App. Third-party services may retain technical information according to their own policies, security requirements, legal obligations, and operational needs. Locally stored photos and edits remain under your device and Android storage controls.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the App. Communications with our configured AI backend use HTTPS encryption. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Lumiere is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using the support contact on the Google Play listing.

12. Your Choices

  • Deny or revoke Android permissions through device settings.
  • Choose whether to use cloud-based AI features.
  • Manage applicable advertising/privacy settings through Android and Google controls.
  • Uninstall the App to stop future app access to permissions and local app data.

13. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used by the App may have their own privacy policies and terms. Their processing is governed by their applicable policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be changed when an update is published.

15. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the Lumiere app owner through the support contact provided on the official Google Play listing.
